# PowerJack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

PowerJack does not collect, sell or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your programs, templates, exercises, workout history and settings are stored on your iPhone. The developer never sees them.

## iCloud Backup

If iCloud Backup is on in Settings, PowerJack keeps a copy of your data in your own private iCloud database, so a new iPhone or a reinstall can restore it. Only your Apple Account can read that copy. Apple handles it under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Turning iCloud Backup off deletes the iCloud copy.

## Live Activities

The rest timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island runs on your iPhone. Nothing about it leaves the device.

## Third parties

PowerJack contains no third-party code, SDKs or services.

## Children

PowerJack is intended only for adults 18 and older.

## Open source

PowerJack is open source, so anyone can check this policy against the code.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

## Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on the PowerJack GitHub repository.
